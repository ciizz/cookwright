# Backlog

Bugs are `B-nnn`, enhancements `E-nnn`; ids are stable and never reused.

How to use it:

- A discovery outside the current task's scope is logged here, not fixed. The bar: a fix that touches files outside the task's declared surface, or more than about five lines outside its scope. Discoveries made while planning count.
- One line per item, dated when opened, in the form `- **B-001** (2026-01-31) What is wrong, where, and how it shows.`
- Closing an item moves its line to Closed with a one-line note. A commit that fixes an item names its id.
